Filipino Flavor Profiles

Core pillars defining regional cooking techniques across the Philippine archipelago.

Tamarind (sampalok), calamansi, green mango, and coconut vinegars serve both as acidic flavor enhancers and natural preservatives tailored for tropical preservation.

Fermented fish sauce (patis) and shrimp paste (bagoong) add deep umami notes to meat stews and vegetable dishes like Pinakbet and Kare-Kare.

Prominent in Bicol and Southern Luzon cuisine, fresh coconut milk (gata) creates rich, velvety sauces spiced with chili peppers (siling labuyo).
